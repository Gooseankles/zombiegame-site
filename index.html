Zombie Dawn

An open-world survival game for a group of friends, on a cold northern coast. Get out of the town, find somewhere quiet to build, stay warm and dry, and in the end clear the zombies and bring the world back.

Pre-alpha: placeholder art, missing pieces, and things that break between versions. Working title.

Download for Windows Looking for the newest version…

Unzip it anywhere and run zombiegame.exe. Start Steam first to play with friends.

When a new version is out, the game says so on its start screen and updates itself with one button.

Roadmap

Where the game is going

First one small village gets every part of the game and is made fun for an evening with friends. Then the world grows.

Built

  • The map12 × 12 km of coast, fjord, valley and mountains, with rivers, lakes and a sea; real sun, moon and seasons
  • Staying aliveHunger, thirst, cold, bleeding and wet clothes
  • LootClothes with pockets, backpacks, searchable furniture; different in every new world
  • The villageTen houses to search, street lamps and a blackout after some days
  • Fire and craftingCampfires, gathering wood and stone, tools and recipes
  • ZombiesThey hear you, chase you and bite; a real model, a crowd in the distance
  • MeleeKnife, hammer, axe
  • Play online or aloneSteam, voice, your own server, several saved worlds, updates
  • Rain clothesRain jacket and trousers keep you dry

Now: finishing the village that holds the whole game

  • Stamina and coldCold and tiredness that change how you play
  • Rarity and wearRare finds, and tools and weapons that wear out
  • Hands and bodiesAnimated arms and bodies
  • A baseTake over a cabin or build one
  • First gunsRare and loud
  • WeatherRain and snow you can see
  • Death and spawnsYour things stay where you fell

After that

  • Signs of the outbreakPopulation signs that change as the world falls apart
  • Zombies at scaleHundreds at once, and worse kinds
  • Bases and powerBuilding in full, generators, getting the lights back
  • The research labClear it, power it, and work towards a cure
  • The rest of the mapRoads, more towns, farms, the dam
  • Military and bunkersHidden places with codes to find
  • Vehicles and a second mapCars and boats, and another place to survive

The goal: find and shut down the source of the outbreak, clear the zombies zone by zone, get power, water and the radio running again, and call in the ship that ends the game.

Zombie Dawn is a working title. A game in development for friends.
