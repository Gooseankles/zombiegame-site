Zombie Dawn · pre-alpha

Vintermark

Twelve by twelve kilometres of fjord, valley, lake and mountain, cut off from the city across the water. Every new world starts in the central valley, in autumn, with the lamps still burning and the snow line on its way down.

The lamps burn at dusk and night until the power fails.

Haldersfield

What is there
    Where you start

    At the edge of Haldersfield or Lundford, in the central valley, with a knife and whatever was in your pockets. The ember dots are the start pockets; the first hour’s walk takes you to the village street.

    How the island opens

    One valley first, then the rest

    The island is built one area at a time, and each new area opens when it holds every part of the game. Names only; what is in them is for you to find.

    1. Open nowCentral valleyHaldersfield, Lundford and the river between them
